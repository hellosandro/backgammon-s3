# Backgammon S3

Pure, simple backgammon: just the board, the checkers and the dice. No frills, truly authentic, with boards and colour combinations to choose from.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the Vercel deployment. Best on a phone held in landscape.

- Tap the dice to roll.
- Tap or drag a highlighted checker; legal squares (each die and their sum) pull it in.
- Tap the dice again to end your turn; the arrow undoes the last step.
- MENU, in the right-hand tray, opens score, new game (vs computer at three levels, or two players), moves and rules.

## Structure

A single self-contained `index.html`: no build step, no dependencies besides Google Fonts (Bodoni Moda, Instrument Sans).
